Part I — Reading

Questions 1–8: Read the text and answer the questions.

A Day at the School Garden

On Saturday morning, Emma and her classmates visited their school garden. Their science teacher, Mr Green, was with them. The weather was sunny, but the air was cool.

The students planted ten small trees and watered the flowers. Emma's favourite plant was a red rose because it had a pleasant smell. Her friend Daniel collected dry leaves and put them into a large box to make compost.

At noon, the students had sandwiches and drank water under a big tree. They were tired, but they were proud of their work. Mr Green told them that trees provide shade and help keep the air clean. The students decided to visit the garden every month.

Before leaving, Emma found a small bird near the fence. It was unable to fly, so the teacher called a local animal rescue centre. The students waited until a rescue worker arrived. They were happy because they had helped both the garden and the bird.

1. What is the text mainly about?

2. Complete the sentence according to the text: Daniel collected dry leaves to ___.

3. Which statement is TRUE according to the text?

4. Which information is NOT mentioned in the text?

5. Complete the sentence according to the text: The students decided to visit the garden ___.

6. Find the word in the text that matches the following definition: Giving pleasure or satisfaction; enjoyable.

v>

7. What adjective is used before the noun rose in the text?

8. Why did the teacher call a local animal rescue centre?
